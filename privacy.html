01

Overview

Virtual Wardrobe ("we," "us," or "our") is an AI-powered personal styling platform built by Shaurya Chauhan and Iti Tanwar. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our website and application.

By creating an account or using our services, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use Virtual Wardrobe.

Plain English summary: We only collect what we need to style your wardrobe. We never sell your personal data. Your clothing images are stored securely in your private account and used only to generate outfit suggestions for you.
02

Data We Collect

We collect information in two ways — what you provide directly, and what is generated automatically through your use of the service.

  • Account Information — Name, email address, and password (encrypted) when you register. If you use Google Sign-In, we receive your name, email, and profile photo from Google.
  • Clothing Images — Photos of tops, bottoms, shoes, and accessories that you upload to build your virtual wardrobe. These are stored in your private cloud storage.
  • Style Preferences — Aesthetic choices, preferred colours, occasion settings, and watch preferences that you configure in the app.
  • Usage Data — Pages visited, features used, outfit combinations generated, and session duration. This is anonymised and used to improve the service.
  • Device Information — Browser type, operating system, and IP address, collected automatically for security and analytics purposes.
03

How We Use It

Your data is used solely to deliver, improve, and personalise the Virtual Wardrobe experience. Specifically:

  • To provide the service — Authenticate your account, store your wardrobe images, and display your clothing in the app.
  • To generate AI outfit combinations — Your wardrobe data and preferences are sent to our AI model (Anthropic's Claude) to produce personalised outfit suggestions.
  • To personalise your experience — Remember your aesthetic choices, saved outfits, and preference settings across sessions.
  • To improve our product — Aggregated, anonymised usage patterns help us understand what features are valuable and what needs improvement.
  • To communicate with you — Send transactional emails (account creation, password reset). We do not send marketing emails without your explicit opt-in.
  • For security — Detect fraudulent activity, prevent abuse, and protect user accounts.
04

AI Processing

Virtual Wardrobe uses Anthropic's Claude AI to generate outfit recommendations and power the AI Stylist chat feature. When you generate combos or send a message to the AI Stylist, we transmit the following to Anthropic's API:

  • Clothing category counts and item names (not the actual images)
  • Your selected aesthetic, colour preferences, occasion, and watch type
  • Your chat messages and questions to the AI Stylist
Your clothing photos are never sent to the AI. Only text metadata (item names and category counts) is transmitted. Anthropic processes this data under their own Privacy Policy and does not use your data to train their models by default.
05

Data Storage

Your data is stored using Google Firebase infrastructure, which includes:

  • Firebase Authentication — Securely manages your login credentials. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plaintext.
  • Firebase Storage — Your clothing photos are stored in a private, access-controlled bucket. Only your authenticated account can access your images.
  • Cloud Firestore — Your wardrobe metadata, preferences, and saved outfits are stored in a secure NoSQL database with per-user access rules.

Google Firebase operates data centres globally. Data may be processed in servers located outside India, including in the United States. Firebase is compliant with ISO 27001, SOC 2/3, and other industry security standards.

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all associated data — including clothing images and wardrobe records — is permanently deleted within 30 days.

06

Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your information with advertisers, data brokers, or any third party for commercial purposes. We share data only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers — Google Firebase (storage/auth) and Anthropic (AI generation). These providers are contractually bound to protect your data and use it only to provide their services.
  • Legal Requirements — If required by law, court order, or government authority, we may disclose information as required to comply with applicable Indian law.
  • Business Continuity — In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale, your data may be transferred to the successor entity, with notice provided to you.
  • Protection of Rights — To prevent fraud, abuse, or harm to users or the platform, we may share data with relevant authorities.
07

Cookies

Virtual Wardrobe uses a minimal set of cookies and browser storage mechanisms necessary to operate the service.

  • Authentication Cookies — Firebase uses session tokens stored in your browser to keep you signed in. These are essential for the app to function.
  • Preference Storage — Your aesthetic and UI preferences may be cached locally in your browser for performance.

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics cookies. You can clear cookies at any time through your browser settings, though this will sign you out of the app.

08

Your Rights

You have the following rights with respect to your personal data:

  • Access — Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction — Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion — Request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.
  • Portability — Request an export of your wardrobe data in a machine-readable format.
  • Withdrawal of Consent — Stop using the app at any time. Deleting your account withdraws all consent.
  • Objection — Object to any processing of your data that you believe is not justified.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 11. We will respond within 30 days.

09

Children's Privacy

Virtual Wardrobe is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

Users between the ages of 13 and 18 should use the service with the knowledge and consent of a parent or guardian.
